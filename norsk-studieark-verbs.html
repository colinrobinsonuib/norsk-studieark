4. Konstruksjoner — Verb Constructions

[verb] for å [verb] — [verb] to [verb] (causality)
til å — (used before infinitive after adjectives)
uten at [subj] [verb] — without [subj] [verb-ing]
ℹ️ These constructions give an idea of continuity (being in the middle of doing something):
å holde på — to be in the middle of (doing)
å ligge og — to be lying and (doing)
å sitte og — to be sitting and (doing)
å stå og — to be standing and (doing)
å drive og — to keep on (doing)
